Survey nearby devices

Profiles BLE advertisements in range and highlights likely solar-camera PMICs (Nordic DFU 0xFE59 / Nordic company 0x0059), ranked by signal strength. Identification only — then connect below. Nothing here is logged.

Survey needs Chrome's experimental Web Platform features — enable chrome://flags/#enable-experimental-web-platform-features and reload. Meanwhile, use "Likely targets only" in the device chooser.

When set, the device chooser only lists devices whose name starts with this text. Leave blank to list every nearby device. The MAC is not visible in-app; identify the unit by the 28:DE:65: prefix in an OS scanner or nRF Connect.

"Likely targets only" narrows the chooser to devices advertising Nordic DFU 0xFE59, Nordic company 0x0059, or your name filter. A device in application mode may not advertise these — switch to "All devices" if the unit is missing.
